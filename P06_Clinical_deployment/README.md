A model score by itself does not change anything: someone needs to decide who is sent to a cardiologist for review.
This practical is a guide to making that decision.

| Part | Content |
|---|---|
| 1 | What a policy costs: outcome counts, and costs in dollars |
| 2 | ROC and precision-recall curves, and the cheapest cutoff |
| 3 | From costs to a threshold probability |
| 4 | Decision curve analysis |
| 5 | Calibration and Platt scaling |
| 6 | A written recommendation, and an optional extension on review capacity |
